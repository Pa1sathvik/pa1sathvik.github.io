04 / Architecture principles

What I optimize for.

The design priorities that connect distributed systems, backend platforms, and agentic engineering.

01

Correctness at the boundaries

Make service responsibilities, API contracts, and state transitions explicit so components remain understandable as a platform grows.

02

Design for the burst

Plan for concentrated demand as well as everyday traffic. Balance performance, scalability, and operational complexity around the application’s workload.

03

Recovery is part of the design

Account for failures and interrupted execution early. Make resilience, recovery, and operational visibility part of production readiness.

04

Make platforms useful to their consumers

Connect shared backend capabilities to the interfaces that need them—from mobile and web experiences to tools used by AI agents.
