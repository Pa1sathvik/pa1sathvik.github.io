PayPal
Member of Technical Staff I · Backend Architect

Backend and architecture across three platforms — communications, decisioning, and server-driven UI.

KAIROS — Communication Decision Platform
  • Designed core components of the platform that governs what every customer is communicated, when, and on which channel.
  • Designed the Intent Management System — the ingestion and management layer for decision intents from producing systems.
  • Architected the Deferred-Intent Scheduler — a durable, leaderless timer on Bigtable and Pulsar for ~25–30M intents/day with 2M-at-once bursts, using per-row atomic compare-and-set for single-fire dispatch and Kafka consumer-group hash-partitioning for coordination without a leader.
  • Owned its low-level design end to end: row-key schema for even distribution, the scan-claim-dispatch loop, garbage collection, recovery, and at-least-once dispatch with idempotent re-evaluation.
  • Designed part of the Ledger and the contracts wiring the scheduler into the decision engine's trigger pipeline.
