Contract / freelance platforms (fastest to start, good for bridging):

Turing.com — specifically matches Indian engineers to US remote roles. Built for exactly your situation. Strong for backend/full-stack.
Toptal — high-end, vetted, pays well; has a screening process but worth it for someone with your experience.
Braintrust — remote engineering, no middleman fees.
Gun.io, Arc.dev (formerly Arc/CodementorX) — remote dev contracts.
Wellfound (formerly AngelList Talent) — startups, many remote, filter for "remote" + "worldwide."
Upwork / Contra — more freelance/project-based; good for quick income, more variable.

Full-time remote job boards (search these with "remote" + your stack):

RemoteOK.com, Remotive.com, WeWorkRemotely.com — dedicated remote boards.
Wellfound, LinkedIn (filter Remote), YCombinator's Work at a Startup (workatastartup.com) — many YC startups hire India-remote.
Himalayas.app, remote.co — remote-first company listings.

The realistic best bet for you: Turing and Wellfound first
